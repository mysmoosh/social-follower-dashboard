Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

SocialFollowerDashboard is a personal desktop application designed to display social media follower statistics and profile QR codes.

Information We Access

With your permission, the application may access your TikTok profile information and follower statistics through TikTok's official API.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to display follower counts and calculate combined follower totals.

Data Storage

Follower statistics and authentication information may be stored locally on the user's computer to support automatic updates. Authentication credentials are not intentionally published or shared.

Third-Party Services

The application may connect to social media platforms to retrieve authorized account statistics.

Data Deletion

Users can stop using the application and remove locally stored application data. They can also revoke connected-app permissions through the relevant social platform.

Changes

This policy may be updated if application functionality changes.
